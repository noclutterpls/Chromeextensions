Clipboard Cleaner

A lightweight Firefox extension that removes empty lines from your clipboard with one click. Perfect for cleaning AI-generated text and messy formatting.

Get the Extension

⚡ What it does

  • Removes empty lines from clipboard text
  • Preserves meaningful content
  • Works instantly with one click
  • No setup or configuration required

🧠 Why it’s useful

AI tools, documents, and websites often add unnecessary blank lines. Clipboard Cleaner instantly removes them so your text is clean and ready to use.

🚀 How to use

  1. Copy any text
  2. Click the Clipboard Cleaner extension icon
  3. Paste clean text anywhere

🔒 Privacy

Everything runs locally in your browser. No data is stored or sent anywhere.
